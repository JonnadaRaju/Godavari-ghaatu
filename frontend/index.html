Our Story

From Amma's Kitchen
to Your Doorstep

It started in a small kitchen in Rajahmundry, where our mother would spend entire mornings hand-grinding spices and slow-cooking the most flavourful pickles in the Godavari delta.

What began as jars gifted to neighbours became a mission — to bring authentic Andhra taste to every Telugu home across India.
